Privacy Policy

GymX Workout Trainer • Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GymX Workout Trainer collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name and email address when you sign in using Google Sign-In.
  • Contact & Feedback: Optional mobile number and feedback messages submitted through the in-app feedback form.
  • Device & Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), device model, operating system version, and general network state.
  • App Interactions & Diagnostics: Workout sessions viewed, exercise completion counts, app crashes, and engagement metrics collected via Firebase Analytics to ensure app stability and improve features.

When We Collect Information

We collect information when you:

  • Create or sign in to your account with Google.
  • Submit feedback through the app.
  • Contact us for customer support.
  • View workouts or interact with advertisements displayed in the app.
  • Receive workout reminders and push notifications.

How We Use Your Information

  • Manage user accounts and authentication via Firebase Authentication.
  • Provide workout routines, tracking, and customized fitness experiences.
  • Deliver non-intrusive workout reminders and notifications.
  • Store feedback securely using Google Sheets.
  • Analyze app performance, fix crashes, and enhance features via Firebase Analytics.
  • Display relevant, compliant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Process and fulfill account deletion requests.

How We Protect Your Information

Your personal information is protected using industry-standard security measures. All communications between the app and external services are encrypted using SSL/TLS technology. Access to your information is strictly limited to authorized personnel and trusted service providers who need it to operate the application.

Third-Party Services

GymX Workout Trainer integrates trusted third-party services to operate the application:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Used for secure user registration and Google Sign-In.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Used to measure app performance, engagement metrics, and crash diagnostics.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): Used to transmit workout reminders and notification payloads.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to serve advertisements within the app.
  • Google Sheets API (Google LLC): Used to securely store user-submitted feedback messages.

These third-party providers operate under their respective privacy policies and data protection commitments.

Advertising & Consent (GDPR, UK GDPR, and US State Regulations)

GymX Workout Trainer displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to keep the application completely free for all users worldwide.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland:
In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google's EU User Consent Policy, users in these regions are presented with a Consent Management Form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You have the choice to consent to, reject, or manage personalized and non-personalized advertising purposes.

Withdrawing or Changing Ad Consent:
You can review, change, or withdraw your consent preferences at any time directly inside the app by navigating to:
GymX App > Settings > Ad Privacy & Consent.

Users in Applicable US States (California, Texas, Virginia, Colorado, etc.):
Under applicable US state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act / CCPA), users have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can exercise this right at any time using the in-app privacy controls.

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access, review, or request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request permanent erasure (deletion) of your personal data.
  • Withdraw any consent previously granted without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent prior to withdrawal.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.

Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, GymX Workout Trainer provides two ways to permanently delete your account and associated data:

1. In-App Account Deletion (Instant):

  • Open the GymX application.
  • Navigate to the Profile tab.
  • Tap Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  • Confirm the deletion prompt. Your Firebase account credentials and local profile preferences will be permanently and immediately removed.

2. Web / Email Deletion Request:

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion remotely, please send an email from your registered Google account email address to:

gymxworkout96@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Data Retention & Deletion Scope:
Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete your authentication record, name, email, profile image link, and associated feedback records from our systems within 7 business days, unless we are legally required to retain specific transaction records.
